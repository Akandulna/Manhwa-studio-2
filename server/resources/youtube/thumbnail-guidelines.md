# Manhwa Recap Thumbnail Guidelines (Channel-Specific)

In the manhwa recap genre, thumbnails are visual storyboards. They must trigger immediate curiosity and show high visual tension.

## 1. Focus on one clear focal point (the 0.5-second rule)

- **Single dominant character:** Feature the main character prominently. Use a high-contrast crop showing an intense facial expression: either looking down in defeat, or displaying an intimidating, overpowered presence (glowing eyes, a dark magic aura, a glowing weapon).
- **The "Before vs. After" or "David vs. Goliath" split:** A classic composition that works exceptionally well:
  - Left side: the weak or beaten protagonist, or someone disrespecting him.
  - Right side: the transformed, god-tier version of the protagonist dominating.

## 2. Minimal, high-impact text (2 to 4 words max)

- **Contrast with the title:** Do not duplicate the video title. If the title is story-focused, use the thumbnail text for the emotional reaction. Style examples: "CHEAT SKILL?", "MAX LEVEL", "HE RETURNED!", "REJECTED", "UNBEATABLE".
- **Legible styling:** Use a bold, thick sans-serif font in a high-contrast colour (bright yellow, neon cyan, or solid white) with a heavy black drop shadow or outline so it stands out against dark anime art.

## 3. Visual polish and colour contrast

- **Vibrant outlines and lighting:** Manhwa art can blend into a dark YouTube background. Add rim lighting, a backglow, or subtle sharpening around the character's silhouette.
- **Avoid clutter:** Do not cram multiple small panels, text bubbles, or speech dialogue into the thumbnail. Mobile viewers will only see messy lines.

## Quick check

One dominant character, glowing eyes or aura where the story has that power, and at most 2–4 bold words.
