# YouTube description instructions (channel-specific)

Write the descriptions for the selected manhwa/manhua recap and its exact chosen YouTube title. Use the verified series research and sources supplied with the request, and research missing facts before writing. Distinguish the manhwa adaptation from a source novel or similarly named work. Each description is shared across all parts of the series.

A recap description has three jobs: keep viewers engaged, help discovery through natural search keywords, and guide viewers through a multi-part story. Build each description in the order below.

## Use easy words (applies to both descriptions)

The audience is Hindi speakers with basic English.

- Use only everyday words a school student knows, and short sentences of about 15 words or fewer.
- Swap hard or fancy words for simple ones: notoriously unforgiving → very hard, disposable → useless, top-tier → best, novice → beginner.
- Avoid idioms. Explain any game or story term in plain words the first time it appears.
- In the Hinglish opening lines, use casual everyday Hinglish, not formal Hindi words.

## 1. Opening hook and summary (first 2–3 lines)

The first 2–3 lines appear before the "Show more" fold on mobile and desktop, so they must carry the hook.

- Summarize the core premise: the protagonist's conflict and what makes him different, matching the selected title.
- In the Hindi-recap version, write these opening lines in natural Hinglish in Roman script (style example only: "Aaj ki video mein dekhiye kaise ek weak aur useless ladke ne payi ek aisi hidden power jisse pura empire kaap utha..."). In the English version, write them in English.
- Reinforce the premise by naming the theme (for example Revenge, System, Magic Academy, Level Up) naturally in the same lines.
- Reveal the premise without resolving the story.

## 2. Attribution and disclaimer

- Do not name the series. Channel policy keeps the real manhwa/manhua name, alternate titles and identifying credits out of the published description. Refer to the work as this story or this manhwa recap.
- Add a short fair-use disclaimer near the end: the video is a recap and commentary made for entertainment and discussion, and all rights to the original story and artwork belong to the original author, artist and publisher. Credit them in those general words only; do not name people, publishers or the work, and do not claim ownership or permission.

## 3. Natural keywords and hashtags

- After the opening summary, write 1–2 short explanatory paragraphs that introduce the protagonist, central conflict and distinctive premise in natural language. Let search phrases such as manhwa recap appear naturally; in the Hindi-recap version, include the phrase "in Hindi".
- Add a brief invitation to subscribe.
- Finish with 3–5 focused hashtags on one line at the bottom. For the Hindi-recap version use tags such as #ManhwaHindiRecap #ManhwaInHindi #HindiRecap; for the English version use tags such as #ManhwaRecap #Manhwa. One or two theme tags are fine. Never use a hashtag containing the series name.
- No tag stuffing: never list comma-separated tags or keyword strings. It violates YouTube's guidelines.

## Final order

1. Hook and summary (2–3 lines)
2. One or two short paragraphs
3. Subscribe invitation
4. Fair-use disclaimer
5. Hashtags

Avoid repetitive keywords and unsupported hype. Do not invent events, part numbers, chapter coverage, credits, links or performance guarantees. Do not add previous-part, next-part or playlist lines. Include channel links only when explicitly supplied. Return the descriptions in the JSON response format specified by the system's description-generation prompt, and keep research evidence separate from the publishable text.
