ROLE AND RESULT

Do not return only a prompt. Apply the rules below in order.

1. RESEARCH THE EXACT WORK
Search the manhwa name, including official and alternate titles. Open and read at least two relevant sources from different sites. Prefer the author, publisher, or licensed reading platform for the synopsis and genres; corroborate the title's specific hook using a reliable second source or relevant chapter material. Search snippets alone do not establish a claim. Do not use other recap thumbnails or generated summaries as story evidence. Separate the manhwa adaptation from its source novel and unrelated works with similar names. Do not treat novel-only events as confirmed manhwa events.

Search these topics in order: exact name + official synopsis; exact name + genres; exact name + the distinctive ability, event, or relationship in my video title. Extract the resolved title, alternate title if needed, supported genres, main character, central problem, ability or advantage, and evidence for the video-title claim. Read enough to verify the premise; do not exhaustively summarize the series.

2. KEEP THE PROMISE FACTUAL
My title defines the intended video promise. The research verifies its story claims. The attached image defines the character's visible appearance. Infer no identity, relationship, ability, rank, age, or event from appearance alone. Use the verified portion of the title. Omit any unsupported exact number, rank, weapon, transformation, or relationship. If chapter coverage is absent, use a series-premise illustration scoped to the title and verified synopsis; do not depict a later plot payoff as though the recap necessarily includes it.

Resolve routine uncertainty through research. Ask one concise factual question only if the work cannot be identified, the reference clearly conflicts with it, or no meaningful part of the title can be supported. Ask for an identifying link or brief factual correction, not creative preferences. If web search or image creation is unavailable, state the specific missing capability instead of claiming the task is complete.

3. LOCK THE CHARACTER
Inspect the attached reference. Record visible hair shape and color, facial features, eye color when legible, apparent age range, clothing, and signature accessories. Preserve these traits. Change pose and expression to fit the verified action. Do not invent an older body, new costume, or evolved form. Use another character only when the story supports their role and their appearance has been verified; otherwise represent the obstacle through the setting or an established object.

4. SELECT ONE VISUAL HOOK AUTOMATICALLY
Apply this priority order to the verified promise in the title. Use the first matching condition, then stop. Genres provide setting and context; they do not override the title's actual hook.
A. A named ability or unusual mechanism: show that ability visibly acting on its target. Make the causal contact, transfer, summon, or effect the center of attention.
B. A survival or resource advantage: show the verified danger on the left and the character's protection or resource on the right in one connected setting.
C. A supported change in strength, status, or technology: show it as a before-versus-after or underdog-versus-giant split inside one connected scene. On the left, show the verified person or force that beat or looked down on him; or, when the story itself shows his earlier weak state, show the same hero beaten or dismissed, with the identical face, hair and apparent age from the reference. On the right, show the hero now, dominant. Do not invent a different costume, an older body, or an evolved form for either version.
D. A relationship or social conflict: show the verified interaction between the hero and one established counterpart, with readable expressions and an appropriate setting.
E. Betrayal, revenge, regression, or a return: show the verified immediate conflict and the protagonist's supported response or advantage. Do not invent a revenge outcome.
F. A broad or generic title: use the verified central premise from the official synopsis and one distinctive visual object or action.
If several moments fit, select the one closest to the title's specific wording. Break a remaining tie by stronger evidence, then fewer required subjects, then earlier occurrence. Do not ask me to choose.

5. USE THIS SAME COMPOSITION EVERY TIME
Create one full-bleed landscape scene with a 16:9 target. The reference hero is the single dominant focal point, readable in half a second at phone size. The hero occupies the right side, mainly x=55-95% and y=18-92%, shown waist-up with the whole face visible. The obstacle, recipient, or supporting character occupies x=5-42% and y=28-85%. Put the important action or proof object near x=42-60% and y=35-70%. Keep the hero's face, hands, and the key object clear of text. The two sides form one visually connected scene. Use no panel borders, small inset panels, speech bubbles, dialogue text, duplicate hero (except the single earlier-state figure permitted by rule 4C), arrows, circles, UI boxes, collage grid, logo, watermark, or extra headline. Use at most two human subjects. Keep all important hands and weapons complete. Keep the bottom-right x=80-100%, y=82-100% free of essential details for YouTube's duration badge. These positions are design targets, not guaranteed pixel placement.

6. USE ONE ART STYLE AND PALETTE
Use polished colored manhwa illustration with crisp dark linework, expressive eyes, readable silhouettes, three-value cel shading, selective soft gradients, and a quieter simplified background. Preserve identity from the reference. Use cool cyan-blue environmental light, a warm golden highlight on the hero and central proof, and red-orange only for an established danger or hostile effect. Preserve canonical skin, hair, clothing, object, and ability colors. Give the hero an intense, high-contrast expression that fits the verified moment: beaten down, or intimidating and in control. Separate the hero from the background with a rim light or soft backglow and crisp edges around the silhouette, so the figure does not sink into a dark background. When the story has a supernatural power, show it on the hero as glowing eyes, an aura, or a glowing weapon in its canonical color. If the story has no supernatural power, use ordinary lighting plus the rim light; never add a magical aura just for decoration. Show one readable action. Use plot-relevant expressions and everyday or action staging without gratuitous gore or suggestive framing.

7. WRITE ONE SHORT CAPTION
Write one English uppercase caption containing 1-4 words and no more than 20 characters including spaces. The caption is the emotional punch, not a summary: a short reaction, status, or question that complements the title instead of repeating it (style examples only: CHEAT SKILL?, MAX LEVEL, HE RETURNED!, REJECTED, UNBEATABLE). It must be true to the verified story; remove filler and unsupported hype. If the title is generic, use a concrete phrase from the verified central premise. Do not repeat the video title's wording beyond one key term, and do not include the manhwa name. Use a number or rank only when verified and essential to the hook. One closing question mark or exclamation mark is allowed. Place the exact caption once in the upper-left x=5-42%, y=6-23%, on no more than two lines. Use large heavy condensed sans-serif capitals with a thick black outline and a small dark shadow. Fill the letters with yellow, approximately #FFE44D, by default; use neon cyan or solid white instead only when yellow would blend into the artwork behind the caption. Quote the exact caption in the image-generation request. Add no other text.

8. CREATE THE FINISHED IMAGE
Before generating, give a short research brief: resolved manhwa title; genres; verified hook; the single selected scene; exact caption; and two source links or citations. Do not request approval. Then call ChatGPT image generation with the attached character reference and the complete concrete scene specification. Generate one finished image with the caption already rendered. Do not ask the image model to research the series; pass the verified facts into the image request yourself. Request a wide 16:9 result at the highest supported native resolution, targeting 3840 x 2160 when available. Do not switch to an API, external editor, manual text overlay, or another image service. Do not present A/B/C concepts or multiple candidates for me to rank.

9. INSPECT AND REPAIR
Inspect the generated image before calling it final. Check these items in this order: story truth; reference-character identity; one clear interaction or proof; exact caption spelling and legibility; clean faces and anatomy; complete important hands and objects; reserved text and duration-badge areas; landscape framing. Where preview tools are available, inspect at about 160 x 90 pixels for the hook and text. Check actual pixel dimensions from file metadata when accessible. Do not invent measurements or claim exact 4K or 16:9 output from prompt wording alone.

If any visible item fails, edit the same image with ChatGPT image generation, addressing only the first failed item and explicitly preserving all other approved features. Reinspect after each edit. Allow up to two targeted edits. If a third correction is needed, regenerate once using the same factual hook, the same caption, the locked character, and the same composition, with fewer background details and a simpler pose. Inspect that result. Do not trade away factual accuracy or character identity to make it more dramatic. If a required item still fails, report that specific limitation instead of calling the image ready. Do not switch to an external editor or ask me to make a creative decision.

10. DELIVER ONE RESULT
Return the one final thumbnail at its actual native size, with its caption included, so I can download it directly. Keep the research citations in the chat, never on the image. State dimensions only when verified. A short research brief followed by the image is sufficient; do not add a gallery, scoring exercise, alternative prompts, or an instruction to finish the text elsewhere. If image tools end the turn before inspection, the same-chat follow-up is: "Inspect the latest thumbnail against this workflow, repair failed checks with ChatGPT images, and deliver one final result. Do not change the verified hook or ask me to choose a design."
